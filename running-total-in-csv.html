How to calculate a running total in a CSV

Revenue to date, a balance after every transaction, sign-ups so far this month. A running total adds each row to everything before it. In a spreadsheet that means a formula dragged down a column; in SQL it is one expression. Synth loads your CSV into a SQLite database in your browser, so you can run it right away. Nothing is installed, and the file never leaves your computer.

The example file

Say you drop a sales.csv like this into Synth. It becomes a table called sales:

day        | store | amount
2026-03-01 | North | 120
2026-03-01 | South | 80
2026-03-02 | North | 95
2026-03-02 | South | 210
2026-03-03 | North | 60
2026-03-03 | South | 45
2026-03-04 | North | 300

Synth keeps CSV values as text, so every query below wraps amount in CAST(... AS REAL) to add it up as a number.

A running total down the whole file

SUM(...) OVER (ORDER BY ...) adds up every row from the first one to the current one:

SELECT day, store, amount,
       SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL)) OVER (ORDER BY day, store) AS running_total
FROM sales
ORDER BY day, store;
day        | store | amount | running_total
2026-03-01 | North | 120    | 120.0
2026-03-01 | South | 80     | 200.0
2026-03-02 | North | 95     | 295.0
2026-03-02 | South | 210    | 505.0
2026-03-03 | North | 60     | 565.0
2026-03-03 | South | 45     | 610.0
2026-03-04 | North | 300    | 910.0

The store in ORDER BY day, store matters. With ORDER BY day alone, the two rows for March 1 both show 200, because rows that tie on the sort column are added in one step. Give the sort a second column so every row gets its own total.

A running total for each group

PARTITION BY starts the total again for every store:

SELECT day, store, amount,
       SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL)) OVER (
         PARTITION BY store
         ORDER BY day
       ) AS running_total
FROM sales
ORDER BY store, day;
day        | store | amount | running_total
2026-03-01 | North | 120    | 120.0
2026-03-02 | North | 95     | 215.0
2026-03-03 | North | 60     | 275.0
2026-03-04 | North | 300    | 575.0
2026-03-01 | South | 80     | 80.0
2026-03-02 | South | 210    | 290.0
2026-03-03 | South | 45     | 335.0

Daily totals with a running total

To get one row per day, group by day first and run the total over the daily sums. The SUM(SUM(...)) reads oddly but is correct: the inner sum totals each day, and the outer one runs across days:

SELECT day,
       SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL)) AS daily_total,
       SUM(SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL))) OVER (ORDER BY day) AS running_total
FROM sales
GROUP BY day
ORDER BY day;
day        | daily_total | running_total
2026-03-01 | 200.0       | 200.0
2026-03-02 | 305.0       | 505.0
2026-03-03 | 105.0       | 610.0
2026-03-04 | 300.0       | 910.0

A moving average

ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW limits the window to the current day and the two before it, which gives a 3-day average:

SELECT day,
       SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL)) AS daily_total,
       ROUND(AVG(SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL))) OVER (
         ORDER BY day
         ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW
       ), 1) AS avg_3_day
FROM sales
GROUP BY day
ORDER BY day;
day        | daily_total | avg_3_day
2026-03-01 | 200.0       | 200.0
2026-03-02 | 305.0       | 252.5
2026-03-03 | 105.0       | 203.3
2026-03-04 | 300.0       | 236.7

Percent of the final total

Divide the running total by the grand total, OVER () with nothing inside, to see how far along you are:

SELECT day,
       SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL)) AS daily_total,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL))) OVER (ORDER BY day)
             / SUM(SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL))) OVER (), 1) AS pct_of_total
FROM sales
GROUP BY day
ORDER BY day;

That shows 22.0, 55.5, 67.0 and 100.0. Click Download CSV above any result to save it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a running total to a CSV?

Drop the CSV into Synth and use SUM(CAST(amount AS REAL)) OVER (ORDER BY date_column). Each row gets the total of every row up to and including it.

How do I restart the running total for each group?

Add PARTITION BY inside OVER, for example OVER (PARTITION BY store ORDER BY day). The total starts again from zero for every store.

Why do rows on the same day get the same running total?

When several rows share the ORDER BY value, SQL adds them together in one step. Add a second column to ORDER BY, such as an id, to step through them one at a time.

How do I calculate a moving average?

Use AVG(...) OVER (ORDER BY day ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW) for a 3-row average. Change 2 to set the window size.

Why use CAST in these queries?

Synth keeps CSV values as text. Casting to REAL makes sure the values are added and compared as numbers.
