Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy covers Synth at synth-sql.com, including Synth Learn and Synth Enterprise. Synth is run by SPE ("we", "us"). Synth BI at bi.synth-sql.com has its own privacy policy.

The short version

  • Your files are loaded and queried inside your browser. They only reach our servers if you sign in and choose to save them.
  • The AI assistant gets your question and your tables' structure (names and column types), not your rows.
  • We count anonymous usage stats only if you allow them.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.
  • You can download or delete everything in your account from Account Settings.

How your files are processed

Your CSV or JSON file is loaded into a SQLite database that runs inside your browser tab, using WebAssembly. Queries, charts and exports are all made on your device. If you don't sign in and save, the file and its contents aren't sent anywhere and are gone when you close the tab.

The AI assistant

The AI features need an account. When you use them, we send the following to our AI provider, Groq, to generate the reply:

  • Assistant: your message, your tables' names, column names and types, row counts, any table relationships you've confirmed, and the earlier reply you're responding to, if any.
  • Chart suggestions: the SQL of the query you're charting, its row count, and its column names, types and number of distinct values.
  • Learn tutor: the lesson, your most recent query for it and the result of your last answer check. Lessons use Synth's built-in practice datasets, not your files.

Your row data isn't included. Anything you type yourself is, though: if you paste data into a message or write values into a query, that text is sent.

Replies from the assistant and tutor are written by AI and are labeled that way in the app. They can be wrong.

We don't use your files, queries or conversations to train AI models, and we don't let anyone else do so. [check Groq's current API terms and data retention for this account, then state here whether Groq keeps or trains on prompts.]

Cookies, local storage and usage stats

Needed to work: if you sign in, a sign-in cookie keeps you signed in across synth-sql.com and bi.synth-sql.com. Your browser's local storage keeps your settings, saved queries, Learn progress and your answer to the usage stats question. None of this is used for tracking or advertising.

Usage stats, only if you allow them: the first time you visit, we ask whether we can count anonymous usage stats. If you allow it, we store a random ID in your browser and record, at most once a day each: a page visit, running a query, creating an account, and opening Learn, checking an answer, finishing a lesson or course, and using the AI tutor. Each record holds the event, the random ID, the page path, the lesson or course id for Learn events, the site that linked you here (its domain only) and any campaign tag in the link (like ?ref=). It never includes your name, email, queries, file names or data. We also load Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. If you don't allow it, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, none of this happens.

We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party tracking scripts, and we don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

How long we keep data

  • Your account and anything you've saved: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • AI request times: 30 days.
  • Anonymous usage stats: 13 months.
  • Data in your browser's local storage stays on your device until you clear it.

Deleted data can stay in our providers' backups for a short time before it's overwritten.

Your choices and rights

From Account Settings (click your email at the top of the app) you can:

  • Download my data: a file with your account details and everything saved to your account, including your uploaded files.
  • Delete account: permanently deletes your account, saved workspaces, uploaded files, chat sessions and Learn progress, plus any dashboards saved in Synth BI, which uses the same account.

You can also delete single workspaces and chat sessions any time. If you'd rather, or if you want to correct something, object to how we use your data, or ask what we hold, email us at synth.sql@gmail.com. We'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for, to keep it secure and working (for example, rate limits), and, for usage stats, only with your consent, which you can withdraw any time with the button above.

Children

You must be at least 13 to create a Synth account, and Synth isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it. [if schools or teachers will use Synth Enterprise or Learn with students under 13, talk to a lawyer about COPPA and school consent before allowing it.]

Security

Saved data is only readable by your account. If you're in a Synth Enterprise organization, other members can see your email address, and its admins and your workroom manager can also see how many AI requests you've made. They can't see your saved files or chats. Connections use HTTPS, and passwords are stored hashed by our sign-in provider. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we'll update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll let account holders know by email or in the app before it takes effect.
