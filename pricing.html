Frequently asked questions

Is Synth free to use?

Yes. The free tier lets you query a single CSV file (up to 50,000 rows) with real SQL or the AI assistant, no sign-in required in Lite Mode.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time payment of $9.99. There's no recurring charge and nothing to cancel later.

Multi-table workspaces (up to 10 tables), table renaming, cross-table AI queries with joins, relationship detection, 5x the daily AI usage limit, and CSVs up to 500,000 rows instead of 50,000.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes. If Premium isn't what you expected, email us within 7 days of purchase and we'll refund you, no questions asked.
